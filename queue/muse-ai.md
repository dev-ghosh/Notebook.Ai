# Muse AI

## Answer first
**Muse** is Meta's personal AI agent, launched on 8 September 2026. Unlike a chatbot, it does tasks for you (email, bookings, shopping, forms, research) on its own cloud computer and keeps working after you close the app. Access it at **muse.ai** or through the Muse iOS/Android app, or WhatsApp. At launch it was available in the **US** only, so users in India may be unable to sign up yet.

> Note: "Muse AI" is also the name of an older, unrelated music-production tool (AI chords, melodies and basslines). This note covers Meta's Muse agent.

## 1. What it is
- A personal AI agent from Meta, built under its AI group led by Alexandr Wang.
- Runs on **Muse Secure VM**: a dedicated cloud virtual machine with a browser and Linux environment that holds the agent and your data.
- Powered by Meta's **Muse Spark** model family (Muse = the agent, Muse Spark = the model).
- You can set its name, avatar and communication style, and watch its browser as it works.

## 2. What it can do
- Manage email and calendar, reminders, plans
- Book appointments and dinners, navigate websites, fill in forms
- Compare prices, track deals, buy with your approval
- Draft reports, proposals, content calendars
- Connect to apps you choose, with access you control
- Keeps running in the background after you close the app

## 3. How to get access
1. Go to **muse.ai**, or install **Muse from Meta** on the iOS App Store / Android Play Store (check availability for your country).
2. Sign in and follow the setup. It can also be used inside WhatsApp.
3. Choose which apps to connect and how much access each gets.
4. Pricing at launch: free tier with a usage meter, plus paid plans at about $20 and $100 per month.
5. Coming later: Meta AI glasses and other devices (Meta also announced a keychain device called Muse Charm).

## 4. Privacy concerns to know
- Some reviewers reported Muse's Mac app syncing local messages data they did not expect, which raised privacy criticism.
- You can opt out of your interactions being used to train Meta's AI models.
- Connect only the apps you need, and review permissions regularly.

## 5. Open questions for you to check
- Whether it is available in India yet (launch was US-only)
- Current plan limits (the free allowance is not a fixed public quota)
