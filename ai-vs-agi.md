# AI vs AGI

## TL;DR
- **AI** is the broad field of making machines do tasks that normally need human intelligence. Everything we use today is AI.
- **AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)** is a *hypothetical* AI that can learn and do **any** intellectual task a human can, flexibly, without being built for each task.

Today's AI is impressive but still counted as **narrow or "broad-but-limited"**. AGI has not been agreed to exist yet.

---

## Simple analogy  
- **AI today** = a team of brilliant specialists. One is great at chess, another at translation, another at writing code.
- **AGI** = one person who can learn to do all of those jobs, and pick up new ones too, the way a human can.

## Side-by-side
| | AI (today) | AGI (goal) |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Strong at certain tasks | Any intellectual task |
| Learning | Needs training data or a lot of examples | Learns new things quickly, like a person |
| Flexibility | Can fail outside what it was trained on | Adapts to new situations |
| Reasoning about the world | Improving, but patchy | Human-level understanding |
| Exists now? | Yes | No agreed example yet |

## Examples
**AI (what you use now)**
- Google Maps finding the fastest route
- Netflix recommending shows
- A chatbot like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini helping you write or code
- A spam filter or face unlock

Chatbots feel general because they can discuss almost anything. But most experts still do not call them AGI: they make mistakes, lack reliable long-term learning, and are not consistently as capable as a person across all tasks.

**AGI (what it would look like)**
- An AI that could learn to be a doctor, then a lawyer, then a software engineer, as easily as a smart human, with no special retraining for each.
- It could handle a totally new problem it has never seen.

## Is there a clear definition?
No. People and companies define AGI differently (for example "as good as a human at most economically valuable work" or "as good as a human at any thinking task"). Because of that, experts also disagree about *when* we might get there.

## One more term: ASI
**ASI (Artificial Superintelligence)** means AI that is far smarter than the best humans at nearly everything. It sits one step beyond AGI and is also hypothetical.

## Quick recap
- AI = the whole field, and what exists today.
- AGI = a future, human-level-and-flexible AI. Not here yet, and the definition is debated.
- ASI = even beyond that.
